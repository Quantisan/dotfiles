Restate the target text more simply and concisely for clarity, without sacrificing technical conceptual precision or terminology. Use your judgment, case by case, on what to cut and how to phrase it.

## Target

- If you are handed explicit text (a draft to restate), restate only that. You may not have the source it came from — that is fine; restate only what is in front of you, and add nothing from outside it.
- If no text is given, restate your previous reply in this conversation.

## Hold these lines

Rephrase the wording; don't rethink the content. Simplifying is where meaning quietly drifts:

- Don't introduce a cause, motive, or implication the original didn't state — never turn "X. Y." into "X, so Y."
- Don't add emphasis or framing the original didn't already make itself.
- Don't drop a qualifier or hedge because it reads like fluff — it may be load-bearing.

Cutting genuinely redundant material is fine; distorting or adding meaning is not.
